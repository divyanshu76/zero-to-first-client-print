About the Author

Divyanshu Verma

Divyanshu Verma is a technology entrepreneur, independent creator, and the founder of DeadCode Labs, a digital technology brand focused on building meaningful digital solutions.

With an interest in technology, web development, artificial intelligence, and automation, he explores how modern tools can help individuals turn their skills and ideas into practical opportunities.

He believes that starting something of your own should not require a perfect background, a large investment, or years of experience. What matters is learning continuously, solving real problems, and taking consistent action.

Through ZERO TO FIRST CLIENT, Divyanshu aims to help beginners understand the journey from having a skill to building a service, finding potential customers, and working toward their first paying client.

His philosophy is simple:

‘You don’t need to know everything to get started. You need to take the first step, learn along the way, and keep building.’
