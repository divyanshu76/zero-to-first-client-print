Have a skill, but no paying client yet?

ZERO TO FIRST CLIENT is a practical, step-by-step handbook for students, job seekers, freelancers and aspiring entrepreneurs who want to turn a skill into a small, focused service business.

Learn how to choose one service by scoring rather than guessing, test real demand, package and price your offer, build an honest portfolio without past clients, find and approach the right prospects, handle discovery calls and objections, and deliver projects with a clear process for feedback, referrals and repeat work.

AI is treated as your assistant, not your replacement: for research, drafting and practice, with a verification habit at every step. Written in Hindi and English, with Indian-market guidance throughout.
