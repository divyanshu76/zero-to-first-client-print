## Permitted changes: technical and print-production refinements only
Each one is to be logged in the table below when made.
1. **Spine width**: replace the 13 mm placeholder with the printer-calculated width once the final page count and paper are confirmed, and re-centre the spine text. *(The spine stays provisional until then.)*
2. **Printer template**: match the printer's exact wrap dimensions, bleed (3 or 5 mm), safe zones and hinge/score allowance.
3. **Colour**: convert to CMYK builds for navy, violet and the ivory ground per the printer's profile; verify on a matte-laminated proof.
4. **Review furniture removed for the print file**: trim and spine guides, the "PROVISIONAL" flag and the review slug are deleted from the printer file.
5. **Barcode**: the box stays an empty placeholder until a legitimate ISBN and barcode are supplied. Then the real barcode goes in this position; nothing is invented.
6. **Print-safety fixes**: if the printer requires it, minimum stroke weights, text outlining, or nudging elements that fall outside the safe zone, without changing the design.
